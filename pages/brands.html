🎬 Video Mode

Static uses Ken Burns pan/zoom on still images. img2vid generates short animated clips from each scene image using AnimateDiff or SVD.

🎞️ img2vid Workflow

CogVideoX — best head/body motion with T5-XXL text encoder. SVD-XT — cinematic, natural motion. AnimateDiff — prompt-guided. IP-Adapter — image-guided + prompted.

💫 Motion Strength 0.5

Lower = subtle motion, higher = dramatic animation. 0.3-0.5 recommended for horror/cinematic.

⚙️ Video Parameters

SVD-XT defaults: 8 fps, 25 frames (~3s clip). AnimateDiff: 8 fps, 16 frames (~2s clip). Clips are looped/trimmed to match scene duration.
