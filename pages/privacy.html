Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Stories That Stalk, operated by Smojo Faceless ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our content creation and social media publishing service ("the Service").

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

When you use the Service, we may collect:

  • Email address (for account authentication via Supabase Auth)
  • Brand configuration preferences

2.2 Social Media Platform Data

When you connect social media platforms, we collect and securely store:

Data Type Purpose Retention
OAuth access tokens Post content on your behalf Until disconnected or expired
OAuth refresh tokens Maintain persistent connection Until disconnected
Platform user/channel IDs Identify where to publish Until disconnected
Channel/page names Display in dashboard Until disconnected

2.3 Content Data

We store content created through the Service including:

  • Generated stories and narration text
  • Generated images and videos
  • Publishing schedules and post metadata
  • Performance metrics collected from connected platforms (views, likes, comments)

2.4 Usage Data

We collect basic usage information such as feature usage patterns and error logs to improve the Service.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to:

  • Publish content — Post videos and associated metadata (titles, descriptions, hashtags) to your connected social media accounts
  • Track performance — Collect engagement metrics from connected platforms to display analytics
  • Improve the Service — Use aggregate, anonymized data to improve content quality and scheduling
  • Maintain security — Detect and prevent unauthorized access

4. Third-Party Platform Integration

The Service integrates with the following third-party platforms via their official APIs:

  • TikTok — via TikTok for Developers (Content Posting API, Login Kit)
  • YouTube — via YouTube Data API v3
  • Instagram — via Instagram Graph API (Meta)
  • Facebook — via Facebook Graph API (Meta)

Each platform has its own privacy policy governing how they handle your data. We encourage you to review their policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google/YouTube Privacy Policy
  • Meta Privacy Policy (Instagram & Facebook)

4.1 TikTok Integration Specifics

When you connect your TikTok account, we:

  • Use Login Kit to authenticate your identity
  • Use Content Posting API to upload and publish video content
  • Store your access token and refresh token securely in our database
  • Access only the permissions (scopes) you explicitly authorize
  • Do not access your private messages, followers list, or personal profile data beyond what is needed for posting

You can revoke TikTok access at any time by disconnecting your account in the Service settings, or by revoking access in your TikTok account settings.

5. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored using Supabase (hosted on AWS infrastructure) with the following security measures:

  • Database-level Row Level Security (RLS) policies
  • Encrypted data at rest and in transit (TLS/SSL)
  • Access tokens stored in a dedicated secure table with restricted access
  • Service-role only access to sensitive credential data

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data or platform credentials with third parties. Data is only transmitted to:

  • The social media platforms you've connected (for content publishing)
  • Our infrastructure providers (Supabase, Render.com) for hosting and processing
  • OpenAI for AI content generation (story text and image prompts only — no personal data or credentials)

7. Data Retention

  • Platform tokens: Retained until you disconnect the platform or tokens expire
  • Content data: Retained for the lifetime of your account
  • Performance metrics: Retained for analytics purposes

Upon disconnecting a platform, all associated tokens and credentials for that platform are immediately deleted from our database.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your stored data by viewing it in the Service dashboard
  • Disconnect any social media platform at any time
  • Delete your account and all associated data by contacting us
  • Revoke platform access directly through each platform's settings

9. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
